01 — The Practice

We built Harbor around a simple idea: continuity is the treatment. Seeing the same doctor for twenty years is worth more than any single visit.

Harbor Family Medical opened on Harbor Street in 2004 with one physician and a shared fax machine. Two decades later we are four physicians and a physician assistant across two offices, and we still cap our panels so appointments run on time.

That means annual physicals that are not rushed, same-day sick visits for our own patients, and chronic care — diabetes, blood pressure, thyroid, cholesterol — managed by someone who remembers what you tried last year and why it did not work.

We handle telehealth for follow-ups and lab reviews, because taking half a day off work to hear "your labs are normal" is a waste of your time.
